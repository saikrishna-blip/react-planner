Lighting plan

  • Hanging brass lantern · in the centre of the cusped arch, the first thing a visitor sees.
  • Pilaster lanterns · 2 brass wall lanterns on the arch pillars.
  • Arch & jharokha uplights · narrow-beam ground spots grazing the marble arch and the carved jharokha.
  • Chhatri glow · a small warm lamp inside each chhatri, so the domes glow on the skyline at night.
  • Eave cove · LED strip under the marble chhajja, washing the sandstone facade.
  • Porch ceiling · 2 downlights in the teak ceiling over the carved door.
  • Gate pillar lanterns · lantern boxes under small domes on both gate pillars, plus a backlit name plate.

Put the outdoor lights on a timer or dusk-to-dawn sensor. All fittings should be outdoor-rated, IP65 or better.
