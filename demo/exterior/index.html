Lighting plan

  • Canopy downlights · 2 × 7 W recessed COB in the porch slab soffit, over the main door and the glass side light.
  • Canopy edge cove · concealed LED strip (IP65) along the front lip of the porch slab.
  • Up–down wall washer · 2 × 6 W on the stone fin, beams grazing the stone texture.
  • Feature frame cove · hidden LED strip under the top band of the tall frame, washing the teak panel and window.
  • Planter spike lights · 3 × 3 W spikes in the raised planter, aimed up at the plants.
  • Gate pillar lights · 2 × cube lights on the compound wall at both sides of the gate.
  • Parapet strip · LED strip under the parapet coping over the entrance bay.

Put the outdoor lights on a timer or dusk-to-dawn sensor. All fittings should be outdoor-rated, IP65 or better.
