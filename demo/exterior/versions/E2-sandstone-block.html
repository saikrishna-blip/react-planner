Lighting plan

  • Soffit cove · concealed LED strip along the front edge of the sloped wood soffit, plus 3 recessed spots.
  • Up–down washers · 2 × 6 W on the stone-block cladding, grazing the texture.
  • Window box glow · LED strip inside the top of the white box frame.
  • Porch ceiling · 2 × 7 W COB downlights in the wood ceiling, lighting the wave wall and door.
  • Side cove line · LED strip under the side parapet coping, the warm line from the reference.
  • Name plate & gate · backlit name plate and cube lights on both gate pillars.
  • Planter spikes · 3 × 3 W spike lights in the front planter.

Put the outdoor lights on a timer or dusk-to-dawn sensor. All fittings should be outdoor-rated, IP65 or better.
